This is a submission for MLH Data Science competition regarding the topic of UC Admissions. We used data that was provided to us by the
organization.

The investigative question that we focused on was: "How do the yield rates (enrolled/accepted) differ for charter vs. non-charter schools 
in the time frame of Fall 2023 to Fall 2025?"

Our timewindow is Fall 2023 through Fall 2025 admissions. Our populations of interest are students from charter schools and students from
non-charter schools. The metric we are measuring is how do yield rates change.

We came up with this investigative question by considering the UC admissions rates of recent years. The significance of yield rates is that
it's one of the factors that determine a school's desirability, financial stability, and prestige. Financial stability means that yield'
rates are part of helping schools accurately budget for housing, staffing, and tuition revenue without over or under admitting.

We found that in this time frame, students from charter schools consistently had a lower yield rate in the UC system than students who 
came from non-charter schools.
